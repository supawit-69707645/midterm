Our Story

Bakery Bakerjai began with a simple dream: to create a special place where an ordinary day could be transformed into a memorable moment through the aroma of freshly baked desserts and flavors that make people want to come back again and again.

From our humble beginnings, we have created a diverse selection of desserts, including Chocolate Cake, Opera Cake, Red Velvet, Matcha Cake, Lemon Meringue Pie, and Peach Tart. Each creation is thoughtfully designed to offer a harmonious taste and its own unique beauty.
